Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026. Policy version: soto-policies-2026-11-06-proposed-final-v1.

1. Business and contact

This policy describes customer information used by SotoALJJ LLC, operating as SotoALJJ Cleaners, for cleaning inquiries, cleaning services, eligible telephone custom-quote consultations and operational communications. Contact sotoaljj@gmail.com for privacy questions, corrections or deletion requests.

2. Information customers provide

Our cleaning and telephone consultation forms request your full name, email address, phone number, preferred contact method, and full service-property address: street, applicable apartment/suite/unit, city, state and ZIP code. The address supports property evaluation and service coverage checks in Cumberland County, New Jersey. It is not the location of a telephone consultation.

Service details include cleaning category, requested service, property type, approximate square footage or an Unsure selection, cleaning needs, company and contact role where relevant, and special instructions. Cleaning inquiries request a preferred service date or timeframe, any specific preferred start time, and service frequency. Optional details include residential bedrooms/bathrooms, commercial rooms or work areas, access considerations, preferred contact time and an alternative phone number.

Telephone consultation information includes eligibility details and, when scheduling becomes available, a requested available date/time, selected appointment slot, verification status and booking or communication records. If you choose operational SMS, the request includes a mobile number and your actual consent choice. Service records may include a request reference, submission timestamp, service history and relevant administrative records.

Do not enter payment-card numbers, door codes, alarm codes, passwords or other security credentials in these forms or in routine email requests.

3. How information supports requested services

We use contact information to respond to inquiries, clarify needs and communicate through your selected permitted method. Property addresses, property details and cleaning requirements support service-area checks, evaluation, scope and individual quotes. Scheduling and communication information supports telephone consultations, approved cleaning bookings, confirmations, reminders, cancellations, rescheduling and service-related updates.

Submitting a request does not confirm a booking. An accepted service record and an approved appointment are separate from your decision about SMS consent.

4. SMS Messaging and Customer Privacy

Each form offers a separate, initially unchecked checkbox for optional operational text messages. SMS consent is not a condition of purchasing services or submitting a request, and is separate from identity verification and service agreements. A phone number alone is not consent. If you decline SMS, Phone and Email remain available; if you selected SMS as your preferred contact method, choose another permitted method.

Operational messages concern requested cleaning services, telephone consultations, appointment confirmations, reminders, cancellations, rescheduling and service-related updates. This program does not authorize marketing messages. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

When authorized online submissions are available, consent records identify the actual affirmative or declined choice, exact disclosure text and version, applicable policy version, recorded time and associated request. An unchecked checkbox must not be recorded as an opt-in. Those records are not currently created by this website's disabled submission controls.

Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance when messaging is available. You may also contact sotoaljj@gmail.com about assistance or messaging preferences. See the Terms of Service for program details. Website SMS enrollment, message delivery and automated STOP/HELP processing are not currently enabled.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile information and SMS opt-in consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

5. Business technology and information sharing

SotoALJJ Cleaners does not sell, rent, trade, or disclose customer information to outside parties for their independent marketing or promotional purposes.

Necessary website hosting and email services may process limited information to deliver those services. Contacting us by email sends the information you include through email services. Providers needed to deliver an authorized requested service may process the relevant information for that service; this is distinct from disclosure for independent marketing or promotional purposes.

This policy does not represent that Google Calendar, Twilio, PayPal checkout or another provider is receiving submissions from these website forms today. Future online intake, verification, scheduling and operational messaging will only be offered when the relevant authorized service is connected. We do not claim that every provider receives every category of customer information.

Online payment is currently disabled on this website; this section does not represent that an active checkout is available or that a payment has been received.

7. Retention and deletion

SotoALJJ Cleaners retains customer service records for up to two years after the customer's last completed cleaning service. Records may be retained longer when required by applicable tax, accounting, legal, dispute-resolution, or preservation obligations.

This service-record schedule does not impose the same deadline on separate payment, tax, SMS consent, opt-out or legally preserved records. It also does not establish a two-year deadline measured from a completed cleaning for inquiries that have never led to a completed service. Deletion requests are handled according to applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping obligations; we do not promise a guaranteed deletion or response deadline.

8. Customer information security

Please provide only information relevant to the requested service and avoid sending security credentials or payment-card details. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We do not promise absolute confidentiality, an unverified security certification or security features that are not operating. The current website forms do not transmit customer details to an intake service or save entered customer information in browser storage.

9. Privacy questions and requests

Contact sotoaljj@gmail.com regarding privacy questions, correction of inaccurate contact/property information or deletion requests. Requests are handled according to applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping obligations. Please identify the information or request involved without including passwords, door/alarm codes or payment-card numbers.

10. Current website availability, effective date and updates

Online form submissions, appointment selection/booking, SMS enrollment and payments are currently disabled. Entering details does not create a stored service request, confirmed appointment, consent receipt or payment. The form code does not send entered customer details to analytics. Website hosting and email remain distinct services; you can contact us by email to discuss cleaning needs.
